# Carry — Product Walkthrough Script

> **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover narration  
> **Total Duration:** 60 seconds  
> **Tone:** Casual, informative — walking someone through the product, not selling it

---

## The Script

**[0:00 – 0:10] INTRO** *(screen: Carry homepage / search bar)*

> So this is Carry — it's a platform that pulls in financial commentary from YouTube, Reddit, and X, and organizes everything by tickers, themes, and predictions. Let me show you around.

**[0:10 – 0:22] SEARCH** *(demo: type "AI semiconductor stocks", show results loading)*

> The main thing is search. You can type something like "AI semiconductor stocks" and it runs a hybrid search — keyword matching, semantic understanding, and it actually classifies your intent. So if you're exploring a sector, it surfaces relevant tickers. If you search a specific stock, it pulls up the narrative around it — predictions, sentiment, all of that.

**[0:22 – 0:35] PREDICTIONS & ACCURACY** *(demo: click into a ticker page, scroll through predictions table, show performance charts)*

> Every prediction we extract gets tracked. So when a YouTuber says "Nvidia's going to $200" — we log that, then check the actual price movement at 1 day, 1 week, and 1 month after the video went up. You can see exactly which calls landed and which didn't.

**[0:35 – 0:48] TICKERFLOW** *(demo: navigate to TickerFlow, show dashboard overview, then ticker detail view)*

> Then there's TickerFlow — this is the social sentiment side. It pulls buzz scores, mention counts, and bullish-bearish ratios from Reddit, X, and news sources. You get a dashboard showing the most talked-about stocks and ETFs, who the bullish leaders are, and you can drill into any ticker to see a signal score with buy and sell markers overlaid on the chart.

**[0:48 – 0:60] CHANNELS & LIVE MONITORING** *(demo: channels page, activity feed with new video notifications)*

> On the channels side, once you add a YouTube channel, Carry monitors it automatically. New uploads get picked up in near real-time, processed through the AI pipeline, and show up in the activity feed. You can browse by channel, by theme, or just use search to find exactly what you need.
