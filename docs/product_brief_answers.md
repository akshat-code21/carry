# Carry — Project Brief

## 1. What is the project about; what are you trying to achieve?

**Carry** turns endless market commentary — YouTube, Reddit, X, StockTwits, news — into structured, searchable, accountable signal. It captures commentary in near real-time, extracts tickers, sentiment, and predictions, lets users ask plain-English questions answered with timestamped clip citations, and **grades every prediction against actual market outcomes**.

## 2. Who will benefit, and the value proposition?

Self-directed retail investors, equity analysts, and finance content professionals. Value: **time** (40 videos → a 5-minute verified read), **trust** (every claim attributed with a clickable citation), and **accountability** (you see who's consistently right, not just loud) — analyst-grade signal at a consumer price.

## 3. Innovation or competitive advantage?

The core moat is the **verified prediction ledger** — no competitor grades public calls against realized prices. AI answers are provenance-first (clips, not vibes); scores are transparent formulas, not black boxes. Commentary, social, news, and smart-money sources fuse in one place. The track record compounds daily and can't be bought or scraped.

## 4. Key milestones (12 months)

- **Q1:** Commentator leaderboard, full prediction grading, methodology page
- **Q2:** Coverage alerts, daily digest, emerging-narrative alerts, invite expansion
- **Q3:** Paid tiers (~$39/mo Pro), demand-driven coverage, public launch
- **Q4:** B2B2C API, 1,000+ paying subscribers, Series-A readiness

**North star:** verified predictions graded per month.
