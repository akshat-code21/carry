# Carry — Product Overview

> **Hear what the market is saying.**  
> Product perspective, based on Carry's current invite-only beta.

## 1. What is the project about; what are you trying to achieve?

**Carry is a market-commentary intelligence product.** It turns the constant flow of financial commentary—from creators, social communities, news, and disclosed investor activity—into research that people can search, understand, and verify.

Today, an investor who wants to understand a stock or market theme may need to watch hours of video, scan multiple feeds, and remember which commentator made which call. Carry brings that work into one place: it identifies the tickers, themes, sentiment, and explicit market views being discussed, then connects each insight back to its source.

The goal is to make market research faster and more trustworthy. Rather than asking users to act on a loud opinion or an unexplained AI summary, Carry helps them answer three practical questions:

- What is the market saying about this company or theme?
- Who made the case, and what was the evidence?
- Which voices have been useful over time?

Carry is a research product, not a trading platform or financial adviser. Its purpose is to help users investigate ideas with better context and evidence.

## 2. Who will benefit from it, and what is the value proposition?

Carry is primarily built for self-directed investors and research-oriented users who want a clearer view of market conversation before making their own decisions.

| Audience | Value to them |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-directed investors** | Replaces hours spent watching videos and following feeds with a concise, source-backed view of the market conversation around a ticker or theme. |
| **Equity researchers and analysts** | Provides a searchable, citable record of what commentators and investors actually said, including the original source and timing. |
| **Finance writers and content teams** | Helps spot emerging themes, understand the narrative around a company, and identify voices gaining credibility or attention. |

The core value proposition is **accessible market intelligence with evidence**. Users can discover the discussion, inspect the supporting source, compare sentiment across inputs, and assess whether past calls were borne out by market outcomes. This gives them a more efficient alternative to fragmented manual research and a more transparent alternative to black-box scores.

## 3. What is the innovation or competitive advantage over other solutions?

Carry's advantage is not simply that it offers sentiment data—many products do. Its differentiation is an integrated, evidence-led research experience.

- **Commentary with receipts.** Insights are tied to named sources and, for video, the exact point at which a claim was made. Users can verify an answer instead of taking an AI summary on faith.
- **Accountability over hype.** Explicit market calls are recorded and reviewed against subsequent market performance. Over time, this helps separate consistently useful commentators from the loudest ones.
- **One view of a fragmented conversation.** Creator commentary, social discussion, news, themes, and smart-money activity can be explored together instead of through separate tools.
- **Explainable signals.** Carry is designed to show the source material and drivers behind a view, rather than presenting a recommendation or opaque score alone.
- **A compounding trust asset.** Its growing history of time-stamped claims and outcome-verified track records becomes more valuable over time and harder for a new entrant to reproduce quickly.

The positioning is deliberately practical: Carry does not claim that no other sentiment or AI research product exists. It combines provenance, verification, narrative context, and accessibility in a way that makes market commentary more useful for everyday research.

## 4. What are the key milestones for the project duration (12 months)?

The roadmap begins from Carry's current invite-only beta and prioritizes proof of trust and repeat use before a broader launch.

| Timing | Product milestone | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Months 1–3** | **Strengthen the beta and trust foundation.** Refine onboarding and the core search-to-source journey; improve coverage quality; add user feedback and correction paths; make methodology and research disclosures easy to understand. | Users quickly reach a useful, verifiable answer and see why Carry is trustworthy. |
| **Months 4–6** | **Make the trust moat visible.** Introduce commentator track records or leaderboards, clearer prediction evidence, curated investor starter coverage, and a channel-request flow driven by user demand. | The product demonstrates who is worth listening to and learns where to expand next. |
| **Months 7–9** | **Create habitual research use.** Launch query-watch, coverage, and emerging-narrative alerts, alongside a concise daily or weekly digest. Expand priority ticker and source coverage where quality and demand support it. | Carry becomes a recurring research habit, not only a tool used for one-off searches. |
| **Months 10–12** | **Prepare a responsible public launch.** Test premium packaging around advanced alerts and smart-money workflows; publish transparent performance and methodology material; complete launch readiness for data quality, disclosures, support, retention, and conversion. | Expand beyond the invite-only beta with a clear value proposition and evidence that users will return and pay. |

Across all four phases, the key product measures should be whether users find source-backed answers quickly, return regularly, engage with citations and alerts, and see enough value in advanced research workflows to pay for them.
